# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead day-to-day delivery execution, team health, and technical planning. They bridge execution realities with product priorities and ensure the team can deliver reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Own team delivery health, staffing, and capacity planning
- Coordinate technical planning, sprint commitments, and work prioritization
- Remove blockers and resolve cross-team dependencies
- Support engineering quality, process consistency, and coaching
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on trade-offs and sequencing

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to translate outcomes into feasible delivery plans
- Partner with Project Managers to align schedules, milestones, and risk reporting
- Support Developers by clarifying priorities, improving flow, and resolving delivery bottlenecks

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution and healthy engineering teams
- Improve delivery throughput without sacrificing quality
- Align technical execution with business goals and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Team planning meetings and engineering check-ins
- Dependency and resourcing discussions with PM and PdM stakeholders
- Escalation updates on risks, staffing, and technical blockers

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers shape usable experiences and help teams turn customer needs into clear, testable product decisions. They ensure solutions are not only built correctly but also work well for users.

### Responsibilities
- Translate product goals and customer insights into user flows and interaction models
- Create or refine design artifacts such as wireframes, prototypes, and usability guidance
- Collaborate with Product Managers to validate assumptions and prioritize experience improvements
- Partner with Developers to ensure implementation matches intended user experience
- Support usability and accessibility reviews before release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to refine user needs, feature direction, and success criteria
- Collaborate with Developers to validate technical feasibility and implementation quality
- Provide feedback to Project Managers on design-related milestones, dependencies, and risks

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and high-value user experiences
- Reduce ambiguity between product intent and implementation
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction through design quality

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery sessions and design reviews
- Partner discussions with PMs and engineering leads
- Validation and feedback loops through usability and acceptance testing

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads ensure projects are designed and delivered with appropriate controls, governance, and secure-by-default practices. They help the team manage risk without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and compliance requirements early in planning and delivery
- Review architecture, integrations, and workflows for risk exposure
- Define controls, review gates, and remediation priorities
- Coordinate with engineering and stakeholders on policy and risk decisions
- Support incident readiness and secure release practices

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to review technical risks and mitigation plans
- Work with Project Managers to ensure security dependencies and controls are included in milestones
- Support Product Managers and stakeholders with risk trade-offs and governance decisions

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the lifecycle
- Build trust in product quality and operational safety
- Make risk decisions transparent and actionable for the full team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Security check-ins during planning and release readiness
- Escalation-based communication for high-risk findings or incidents

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how success is measured and how teams use evidence to improve decisions. They support product and operational learning through metrics, dashboards, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Define product and operational metrics tied to business and customer outcomes
- Build or maintain dashboards, instrumentation, and reporting views
- Help teams interpret usage patterns, quality trends, and adoption signals
- Support experiments, evaluation, and post-launch analysis
- Partner with stakeholders on outcome measurement and continuous improvement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define measurable success criteria and KPI reporting
- Support Project Managers with milestone and release impact tracking
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure instrumentation and analytics requirements are built in from the start

### Goals
- Make performance and impact visible to the team and stakeholders
- Improve decision-making with evidence rather than assumptions
- Help the project course-correct based on measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- KPI definitions and reporting updates
- Review meetings on adoption, quality, and customer impact
- Data-driven discussions during planning and retrospective cycles

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Partners represent the lived experience of users and customers after release. They help the team understand adoption, issues, and product feedback that may not be visible in engineering workflows alone.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback, support trends, and usage pain points
- Surface recurring issues, blockers, and product opportunities to the team
- Coordinate post-release learning and customer-impact triage
- Support stakeholder communication regarding customer sentiment and service quality
- Help prioritize fixes or improvements based on operational impact

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer feedback and support signals with Product Managers to inform prioritization
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release communication and issue tracking
- Work with Developers and QA on validation of reported problems and customer-reported regressions

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure product changes address real user needs and operational realities
- Reduce friction between customer experience and internal delivery processes

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue escalation paths
- Weekly or milestone-based status syncs with product and delivery partners
- Support and adoption updates during releases or after launch

---

## Release Manager / Operations Partners

### Role Summary
Release Manager / Operations Partners coordinate release readiness, deployment execution, and operational continuity. They reduce risk by making sure the team is prepared to ship and support changes smoothly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing, readiness checks, and deployment windows
- Confirm rollout plans, rollback paths, and operational support coverage
- Partner with engineering and QA to validate release criteria and smoke testing
- Communicate release status, known issues, and stakeholder notifications
- Help monitor post-release health and coordinate follow-up actions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to integrate release milestones into project plans
- Partner with Developers and QA on launch readiness and validation criteria
- Coordinate with Product Managers and stakeholders on release communications and known risks

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and operational disruption
- Improve deployment predictability and post-release confidence
- Support a smooth path from code completion to customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch checklists
- Status updates during deployment windows and incident response
- Post-release summaries with lessons learned and follow-up actions
